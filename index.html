How to play

  • The top row is a guide — it shows the colour each column must end up as.
  • Tap any block in the same row or column as the empty slot to slide it in.
  • A whole run of blocks slides at once, so one tap can move several.
  • You win when every block matches the guide colour above its column.
Other ways to move
  • Swipe across the board to push blocks in that direction.
  • Arrow keys work too, and push the same way.
  • One column keeps the empty slot — that colour has one block fewer.
Modes and hints
  • Daily gives everyone the same puzzle each day — solve it to build a streak.
  • Levels is a campaign of 24 puzzles that grow from a nudge to a full scramble. Beat the move par for three stars.
  • Hint shows you one good next move. You get three per puzzle.
Settings and accessibility
  • Mode, difficulty, colours and options live behind the ⚙ button.
  • The bar-chart button in the header opens your lifetime stats.
  • Colourblind? Switch to the Accessible palette, or turn on Symbols.
